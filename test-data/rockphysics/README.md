# Rock physics goldens (G6.0)

Generated by `tools/validation/rockphysics/genfixtures.py` from
`oracle.py` — independent stdlib-Python reference implementations
(dual-implementation rule: the G6.1 JS engines validate against these
files and share no code with them). Regeneration is deterministic and
byte-identical; `main()` self-asserts eleven physics anchors (A1–A11,
listed in genfixtures.py) before writing, so a regeneration that
breaks physics refuses to land.

## Provenance

Every numeric constant in the oracle was cross-checked on 2026-07-13
against open, tested implementations rather than written from memory:

- **Batzle & Wang 1992** brine/water: bruges (Agile Scientific).
  This check caught a real error — the brine-velocity S² coefficient
  is **−820**, not the −1820 sometimes misquoted.
- **Batzle & Wang 1992** gas Z-factor/K and dead/live oil:
  equinor/open_petro_elastic (`material/batzle_wang/`). Live-oil
  density follows that implementation: eq 24 as written (B₀ carries
  the temperature dependence, no extra eq-18/19 correction); live-oil
  velocity is eq 20a on the eq-22 pseudo-density.
- **Greenberg–Castagna 1992** coefficients: rockphypy (sand/shale)
  and auralib (limestone/dolomite, mudrock line).
- **Exact Zoeppritz PP**: the Dvorkin et al. (2014) expression form
  as published in bruges `reflection.py`; anchored to the analytic
  normal-incidence identity, the small-contrast Aki–Richards limit,
  and the vanishing-Vs acoustic (fluid–fluid) coefficient.
- **Tuning**: the Ricker equal-and-opposite-pair tuning thickness is
  the peak-to-trough time √6/(2πf) — Kallweit & Wood (1982)
  approximate it as 1/(2.6·f_dom); the golden (16 ms at 25 Hz, 1 ms
  grid) matches to within one sample.

## Conventions

- SI in the goldens: densities kg/m³, velocities m/s, moduli Pa.
  (Batzle-Wang functions run internally in the paper's native units:
  °C, MPa, g/cc, weight-fraction NaCl, GOR in L/L.)
- AVO curves store exact Zoeppritz (real and imaginary parts),
  Aki–Richards, and 2-/3-term Shuey per angle 0–40°, plus Shuey A/B/C
  and the Rutherford–Williams class (|A| ≤ 0.02 band for class II —
  documented convention; the literature draws that band
  qualitatively).
- The four AVO interface fixtures are constructed shale-over-sand
  cases, self-asserted to classify I/II/III/IV.

## U2 goldens (2026-10-01)

`goldens.u2.json` is generated by `tools/validation/rockphysics/genfixtures_u2.py`
from `oracle_u2.py` (stdlib Python, no code shared with the JavaScript; it
builds on the G6.0 oracle's Zoeppritz, Gassmann, Greenberg-Castagna, Ricker
and convolution). `goldens.json` is not regenerated. `main()` asserts ten
anchors (B1 to B10) before it writes:

- **B1, published AVO example.** van der Baan and Smit (2006, Geophysics 71,
  C93), model 1 of their Table 1 with the lower P velocity at 3.0 km/s
  (Vp 2.40/3.00 km/s, Vs 0.98/1.755 km/s, density 2.2/2.0 g/cc): "a polarity
  reversal occurs twice at incidence angles of 25 and 49 degrees", critical
  angle 53 degrees. The oracle's exact Zoeppritz coefficient crosses zero
  at 25.02 and 49.01 degrees and nowhere else before 53.13 degrees. The
  Aki-Richards linearisation reverses near 21 degrees, so the check
  discriminates.
- **B2.** Ostrander (1984) gas sand under shale (Vp 3048/2438 m/s, Poisson's
  ratio 0.40/0.10, density 2.40/2.14 g/cc): R(0) = -0.1674 and the
  magnitude grows with angle.
- **B3 to B5.** Angle gather: an isolated interface reads the coefficient;
  constant-phase rotation (scipy.signal.hilbert weights as a plain DFT);
  intercept and gradient by least squares of R = A + B sin^2(theta).
- **B6.** Fluid line: Castagna, Swan and Foster (1998), B/A =
  (1 - 4 (Vs/Vp)^2 (g + 2)) / (1 + g); B = -A at Vp/Vs = 2.
- **B7.** Iterative Vs (Greenberg and Castagna 1992 procedure, RPH 7.9)
  recovers the true Vs of a gas sand whose brine state lies on the
  regression; the direct regression is 16 to 26 percent low.
- **B8.** Critical-porosity sand line (Nur et al.): the mineral point at
  zero porosity; Gassmann collapses to the Reuss average at phi_c.
- **B9.** Gardner et al. (1974): rho = 0.23 V^0.25 (g/cc, ft/s), so
  10,000 ft/s is 2.30 g/cc. Faust (1953): V = 1948 (Z R)^(1/6) (ft/s, ft,
  ohm m), equal within 0.01 percent to the 2.2888 (Z R)^(1/6) km/s form
  (Z in km) of Hacikoylu, Dvorkin and Mavko (2006).
- **B10.** Voigt fluid mix is at or above Wood, equal at the end members.

Not validated against a published table: the gather's amplitudes at angles
other than the published reversals (they rest on the oracle), and the Faust
constant's fit to any particular basin (it is calibrated per well in the
Suite and the misfit is reported there).

## Granular models (QI Q2, 2026-10-06)

`goldens.granular.json` is written by `tools/validation/rockphysics/oracle_granular.py`
(stdlib only; asserts anchors A1-A9 before writing; byte-identical on
regeneration). It covers Hertz-Mindlin (both slip conditions), soft sand,
stiff sand, Dvorkin-Nur contact cement (contact and surface schemes),
Avseth constant cement, Hashin-Shtrikman-Walpole bounds and Brie.

Cross-checked on 2026-10-06 against rockphypy (GM.hertzmindlin, softsand,
stiffsand, contactcement, constantcement; Fluid.Brie) with
`crosscheck_rockphypy.py`: worst relative difference 2.4e-15. rockphypy
takes stress in MPa, and its docstring describes f the other way round;
its formula, like ours, gives the classic no-slip Hertz-Mindlin at f = 1.
